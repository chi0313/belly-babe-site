Frequently asked

Where is my data stored?

Everything lives on your iPhone and, if you use iCloud, in your own private iCloud backup. No accounts, no servers — we never see your entries or photos.

How do I back up or move to a new phone?

Your data is included in your normal iPhone backup. Restoring it on a new phone brings your journal with it. You can also export anytime in Settings → Your data.

How do I restore my Belly Babe Club purchase?

Open Settings in the app and tap Restore purchase, signed in with the same Apple Account you purchased with.

How do I delete all my data?

In the app: Settings → Your data → Delete all data. If you'd like a copy first, export before deleting.

Is the weekly content medical advice?

No. Everything in the app is gentle company — for anything about you or your baby, please talk with your doctor or midwife.
