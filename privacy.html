Privacy Policy

Belly Babe: Pregnancy Journal · Effective September 10, 2026

The short version: your journal belongs to you.

No accounts. No servers. No ads. No analytics. No tracking.

Everything stays on your iPhone and in your own private iCloud.

1. What Belly Babe stores

Belly Babe keeps what you put into it: diary entries, moods, photos, checkup notes and measurements, body notes, questions for your doctor, your baby's nickname and due date, and your preferences. All of it is stored locally on your device — we have no way to read or access any of it.

2. What we collect

Nothing. No user accounts, no backend servers, no third-party analytics, no advertising SDKs. We do not collect, sell, share, or process personal information.

3. iCloud

Your data may be included in your device's standard backup, controlled entirely by your own Apple Account. These backups are private to you; we cannot access them.

4. Device permissions

Photos — only when you choose photos to add. Camera — only for in-app bump photos. Notifications — optional gentle reminders, off by default, quieting down automatically after your due date. Each permission is requested only when you use the feature.

5. Purchases

Belly Babe Club is processed by Apple through the App Store. We never see your payment details.

6. Your control

Export anytime (Settings → Your data). Delete everything anytime (Settings → Your data → Delete all data).

7. Children

Belly Babe is intended for expectant parents and is not directed at children under 13.

8. Not medical advice

Content in the app is gentle companionship only — always consult your doctor or midwife about anything concerning you or your baby.

9. Changes

Updates will be posted here with a new effective date. Since the app collects nothing, changes should be rare.

10. Contact

Questions about privacy? Email BellyBabeAPP@gmail.com.
