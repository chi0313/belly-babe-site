BELLY BABE

Privacy Policy

Belly Babe: Pregnancy Journal（肚肚寶貝：懷孕日記） · Effective September 10, 2026

The short version: your journal belongs to you.

No accounts. No servers. No ads. No analytics. No tracking.

Everything stays on your iPhone and in your own private iCloud.

1. What Belly Babe stores

Belly Babe keeps the things you put into it: diary entries, moods, photos, checkup notes and measurements, body notes, questions for your doctor, your baby's nickname and due date, and your app preferences.

All of it is stored locally on your device. We — the developer — have no way to read, receive, or access any of it.

2. What we collect

Nothing. Belly Babe has no user accounts, no backend servers, no third-party analytics, no advertising SDKs, and no crash-reporting services that send us your data. We do not collect, sell, share, or process personal information.

3. iCloud

Your data may be included in your device's standard backup (iCloud or computer backup), controlled entirely by your own Apple Account settings. These backups are private to you; we cannot access them.

4. Device permissions

  • Photos — only when you choose photos to add to your journal. The app never scans your library in the background.
  • Camera — only when you take bump photos with the in-app camera.
  • Notifications — optional gentle reminders, off by default, entirely under your control in the app's Settings. Reminders quiet down automatically after your due date.

Each permission is requested only when you use the related feature, and the app works without them.

5. Purchases

Belly Babe Club (subscription or lifetime purchase) is processed by Apple through the App Store. We never see your payment details. Purchases can be managed and restored through your Apple Account.

6. Your control

  • Export — in the app: Settings → Your data → Export everything (photos + JSON), anytime.
  • Delete — Settings → Your data → Delete all data removes every entry, photo and setting from the device.

7. Children

Belly Babe is intended for expectant parents and is not directed at children under 13.

8. Not medical advice

Content in the app is general information for companionship only — it is not medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment. Always consult your doctor or midwife about anything concerning you or your baby.

9. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this page with a new effective date. Because the app collects nothing, changes are expected to be rare.

10. Contact

Questions about privacy? Email ai@ji0n.com.
